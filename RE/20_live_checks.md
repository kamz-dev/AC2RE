## Session 1 (2026-10-21)
Read-only external reader (`livelab/peek.py`, `livelab/record.py`: `ReadProcessMemory`, no injection), about 118 Hz,
100 s. The sequence was: stand, walk, high profile, high profile + legs, release while running, a 180° swing, turn
on the spot, a vault and a drop. The player is found as `EngineContextImp::ms_Instance` (0x1E10AC0) +0x4C → World;
`World::GetPlayer` (0x3A300) gives the entity: `[[World +0x53C] +4][[0x1E3DBC0] +0x24]`; the Human is
entity +0x74 → +0x54 → +0x2A4, and its HumanGround is the one whose +0x08 is that Human (also Human +0x440). The
recordings stay out of git.
